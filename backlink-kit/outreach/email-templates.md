## Subject Line A/B Testing

For outreach emails, try these subject line variations:

✅ Question-based (highest open rate):
- "Quick question about your [topic] resource page"
- "Question about [their article title]"

✅ Personalized:
- "Loved your piece on [topic]"
- "[Their name], thoughts on this?"

✅ Direct value:
- "Free dataset for your readers"
- "Broken link on [page title]"

❌ Avoid these (low open rate):
- "Link exchange request"
- "Backlink opportunity"
- "Boost your SEO"
- ALL CAPS subjects

## Email Sending Rules

1. **Personalize 100%** — Generic emails fail. Spend 5 min on each.
2. **Send 5-10 per day** max — Anti-spam protection
3. **Use real signature** with photo, LinkedIn link
4. **No tracking pixels** — they're detectable and creepy
5. **Follow up ONCE only** at 7 days
6. **Track replies** in a Google Sheet
7. **Test from different addresses** if reply rate is 0%

## Expected Response Rates

- Resource page outreach: **15-25%** reply rate
- Guest post pitches: **5-10%** acceptance
- Broken link emails: **30-40%** thank-you replies (5-10% link added)
- Podcast pitches: **10-15%** booking rate
- Journalist pitches: **3-5%** story published
- Cold professor emails: **20-30%** reply rate
