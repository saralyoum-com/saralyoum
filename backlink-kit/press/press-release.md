# sardhahab.com Launches First Comprehensive Arabic Gold Price Tracker Supporting 19 Currencies and New Official Saudi/UAE Symbols

**Real-time Arabic-language platform addresses $1.5 trillion gap in MENA gold market data accessibility**

**[CITY], [DATE]** — sardhahab.com, a real-time Arabic gold and currency price tracking platform, today announced support for all 19 Arab currencies and full implementation of the new official Saudi Riyal (SAR) and UAE Dirham (AED) symbols introduced by SAMA and CBUAE in 2024.

The platform addresses a significant gap in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) financial services market, where most existing gold-tracking tools display prices only in US dollars or omit Arabic currencies entirely.

"For decades, 400 million Arabic speakers have managed gold investments using tools designed for Western markets," said Ayman Maloustat, founder of sardhahab.com. "When 65% of users opt for USD pricing simply because their local currency display feels unprofessional, that's not a preference — it's a gap in the market."

## Key Features

The platform offers:

- **Real-time gold price tracking** in 19 Arab currencies including SAR, AED, KWD, QAR, BHD, OMR, EGP, JOD, MAD, IQD, TND, DZD, LBP, LYD, SDG, YER, SYP, ILS, and USD
- **Dedicated country pages** for 16 Arab nations with culturally-relevant local context
- **Free zakat calculator** supporting all gold karats (24K, 22K, 21K, 18K, 14K)
- **Chainlink-verified pricing** for transparency and trust
- **Price alerts** via email and push notifications
- **Multi-asset tracking** including silver, Bitcoin, and Ethereum
- **Official currency symbols** for SAR and AED (first major Arabic finance site to do so)

## Market Context

The Arab gold market represents approximately $1.5 trillion in personal and institutional holdings, according to combined data from the World Gold Council and central banks. Per capita gold consumption in Gulf countries is 8 times the global average.

"Arabic-speaking users were getting second-class digital experiences when it came to gold tracking," Maloustat explained. "We built sardhahab.com to be Arabic-first, not Arabic-translated."

## Technical Implementation

The platform uses:
- Next.js 14 with edge middleware for Arabic URL handling
- Vercel global CDN for sub-second load times in MENA
- Chainlink decentralized oracles for price verification
- Supabase for alert management
- Resend for email notifications

The site supports 50+ pages with real-time data, all served in under 1 second to Arabic-speaking users.

## Availability

sardhahab.com is freely available worldwide at [https://sardhahab.com](https://sardhahab.com). No registration is required for basic price tracking. Users can subscribe to free email alerts through the platform.
