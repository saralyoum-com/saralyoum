## ⚠️ Things to Avoid

### Black Hat Tactics (NEVER do these)
❌ Buying backlinks (Fiverr, etc.)
❌ Private Blog Networks (PBNs)
❌ Comment spam
❌ Forum signature spam
❌ Hidden links
❌ Link farms
❌ Cloaking
❌ Spammy guest posts on irrelevant sites

### Gray Hat Tactics (Risky)
⚠️ Excessive outreach (>20 emails/day)
⚠️ Reciprocal link schemes
⚠️ Article spinning
⚠️ Fake reviews/testimonials

### Wikipedia Don'ts
❌ Multiple edits per article in one day
❌ Promotional language
❌ Creating articles about yourself
❌ Using multiple accounts
